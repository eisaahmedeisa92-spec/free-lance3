About the work

Freelance, not an agency queue

Who I am

I'm an independent web developer. Every project that comes through this site is scoped, built, and delivered by the same person you talk to from the first message — no hand-offs, no account managers relaying information.

What I build

Clean, functional websites in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — from a simple one-page site to a multi-page project with forms, layouts, and interactive pieces. The scope is set upfront using the tiers on the right.

Who it's for

Clients who know roughly what they need and want a straight answer on cost, timeline, and what "done" looks like before committing to anything.

Our policy

What you can expect, in plain terms

Scope first

Every job is classified as Easy, Medium, or Hard before work starts, based on the page count and code-length rules explained on the Guidelines page. That classification decides the price and timeline — there are no surprise additions mid-project.

One point of contact

All communication happens by email and phone directly with the developer. Call or message 01030340178 for anything that can't wait for a reply.

Delivery

The finished site is sent to the email address supplied on the request form. Keep that address correct and check it once your project's estimated timeline has passed.

Revisions

Reasonable revisions that stay inside the original request are included. Changes that grow the scope move the project to a higher tier and are quoted separately.
